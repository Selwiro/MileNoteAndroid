MileNote里程志

A simple, practical app for keeping a car's books on Android. Manage several vehicles, log everyday costs, analyse fuel and electricity use, plan maintenance, and see what running the car really costs.

No account to create. The ledger is stored on your device by default, and can sync between your own devices over WebDAV.

Requires Android 8.0 or later·Free·No account

What it does

  • Everyday entries, made easy

    Log fuel, charging, parking, insurance and repairs. Custom categories, search and filters, plus receipt scanning, keep routine bookkeeping quick.

  • Several vehicles, kept apart

    Add more than one vehicle and manage its costs, energy use and service history separately. Data never mixes, and switching vehicles takes one tap.

  • Fuel and electricity, clearly measured

    Petrol cars use the full-tank method to work out real fuel economy; EVs use the full-charge method for energy use. See average consumption, energy cost per kilometre and how it changes over time.

  • Costs at a glance

    Expenses are summarised automatically, with monthly trends and a breakdown by category, so long-term running costs are easy to follow.

  • Maintenance that shows up in time

    Service items are tracked by date and mileage; whichever comes first puts the item on your to-do list. Completing a service schedules the next one.

  • Your data comes and goes with you

    Import records from apps such as 小熊油耗 and 钱迹, export CSV, keep a complete JSON backup, or sync over your own WebDAV.

Screens

A calm, uncluttered interface

Built with Material 3 Expressive, with five colour schemes, light and dark themes, and a Chinese or English interface.

Privacy and data

Your data stays on your device by default

No ads, no subscriptions, no analytics.

WebDAV sync needs your own storage service; the remote ledger is stored as plain JSON.

  • No accountNothing to register or sign in to.
  • Stored locallyThe ledger lives only on your phone by default.
  • On-device processingReceipt scanning and file parsing happen on the device.
  • Your own backupsExport the complete ledger at any time — no developer server involved.
  • Optional syncUse your own WebDAV service to move data between devices.
