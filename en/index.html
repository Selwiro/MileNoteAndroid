MileNote is a small, practical app for looking after your car: several vehicles kept apart, real fuel economy measured at the pump, maintenance that shows up before it's due, and running costs you can actually read.

No account to create. Your data stays on your device by default, and can sync between your own devices over WebDAV.

Features

Six things it takes care of

From the first fill-up to the yearly report, all of it happens on your phone — no connection needed.

  • Everyday entries, made easy

    Log fuel, charging, parking and repairs, with custom categories and receipt scanning.

  • Several vehicles, kept apart

    Each vehicle keeps its own costs, energy use and service history.

  • Fuel and electricity, clearly measured

    Full-tank and full-charge methods show real consumption and trends.

  • Costs at a glance

    Monthly trends and category breakdowns show where the money goes.

  • Maintenance that shows up in time

    Due by date or mileage; finishing one schedules the next.

  • Your data comes and goes with you

    Import from other apps, export CSV / JSON, or sync over your own WebDAV.

Privacy and data

Your data stays on
your device by default

No ads, no subscriptions, no analytics. No server is collecting your trips or fill-ups.

WebDAV sync needs your own storage service; the remote data is stored as plain JSON.

  • No accountNothing to register or sign in to.
  • Stored locallyYour data lives only on your phone by default.
  • On-device processingReceipt scanning and file parsing happen on the device.
  • Your own backupsExport a complete backup at any time — no developer server involved.
  • Optional syncUse your own WebDAV service to move data between devices — nothing passes through us.
